Privacy Policy

Bench · Version 1.0 · Effective 5 September 2026

Bench has no servers and no accounts. It does not collect, transmit or store any personal data about you.

The short version

Bench runs entirely on your device. There is no Bench server for it to talk to, no account to create, and no analytics or advertising code in the app.

We do not collect your personal data. We cannot see your boards, your panels, the readings from your sensors, or how you use the app.

What the app stores

Bench saves the following in its own local storage on your device, so your panels are still there next time you open it:

  • The boards you have added, including the name, address and port you entered
  • The list of variables each board reported when it was last connected
  • The panels you have built and the widgets on them
  • Your preference for haptic feedback
  • Whether you have purchased the paid unlock

What leaves your device

When you open a panel, your device opens a network connection directly to the board address you entered. That traffic goes to your board and nowhere else. It does not pass through any server operated by us.

When you use the board discovery feature, your device sends connection attempts to addresses on your local network in order to find boards that respond. Those attempts stay on your local network.

Live values, control commands and log lines travel only between your device and your board.

Purchases

The paid unlock is sold and processed by Google Play. We never receive or handle your payment details.

Google processes that transaction under its own privacy policy, and may collect data in doing so. Please read Google Play’s privacy policy for details of what Google collects.

The app stores only a simple flag recording that the unlock was purchased.

Permissions the app asks for

Bench requests these Android permissions, and uses them only as described:

  • Internet and network state: to open the connection to your board on your local network
  • WiFi state and multicast: to discover boards that announce themselves on your network
  • Vibrate: to give haptic feedback when you operate a control
  • Billing: to offer the one-time paid unlock through Google Play

Third parties

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Google Play is the only third party involved, and only for distributing the app and processing the optional purchase.

Children

Bench is a tool for electronics hobbyists and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is local. Open Settings and choose Reset all data to erase your boards and panels, or uninstall the app to remove all of it at once.

There is nothing stored elsewhere for us to delete, because nothing was sent anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what the app does with your data, the updated version will ship with an app update and the effective date at the top will change.
